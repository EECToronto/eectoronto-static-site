Welcome to the Ethiopian Evangelical Church in Toronto, where we are committed to building a community of believers in Jesus Christ. As a church that is primarily made up of Ethiopians and Eritreans, we strive to create a welcoming environment that reflects our unique cultural heritage while also celebrating our shared faith.

If you are interested in becoming a member of our church, please fill out our registration form. Your personal information will be kept confidential and used only for church-related purposes.

Our church offers a variety of opportunities for worship, fellowship, and service, including:

- Amharic and English language services

- Bible studies and small group gatherings

- Youth and children’s ministries

- Women’s and men’s ministries

- Outreach programs to the Ethiopian and Eritrean communities in Toronto

We believe that everyone is called to serve in some capacity, and we encourage all members to use their unique gifts and talents to build up the church and serve others.

Thank you for considering the Ethiopian Evangelical Church in Toronto as your spiritual home. We look forward to getting to know you and sharing in the joy of fellowship and worship together.
